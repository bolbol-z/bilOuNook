NOOK LAYOUT SPEC (points; physical-notch Mac: notch 179x32, menu bar 36)
- Open size: width 420 (media 195 + divider 1 + calendar 224), height = notchHeight + 2 + 75 + 9 = 118. Minimum page width 300 when fewer widgets are enabled. Open bottom corner radius 20 (closed radii stay calibrated to the hardware notch). Top flare and shadow as today.
- Top band (height = notchHeight): tab pills left-aligned at x = 9 (they do not hug the notch); gear right-aligned 12 from the right edge; both vertically centered in the band; nothing enters the forbidden zone.
- Pill: height 20, capsule, horizontal padding 6, icon 12 + gap 4 + label 10.5 semibold; gap between pills 6. Selected: fill white 14%, label and icon white 100%. Unselected: no fill, white 85%. Icons: lamp.desk.fill (Nook), tray.fill (Tray). Gear: gearshape.fill 12, white 70%.
- Content row: top = notchHeight + 2, height 75, bottom padding 9. Widgets are separated by 1pt dividers (75 tall, white 10%).
- MEDIA WIDGET (width 195): left padding 9; artwork 75x75 radius 11; source badge 20x20 (radius 5) whose bottom-right corner is 2.5 outside the artwork's bottom-right corner; text column x = 93, max width 102, right padding 8. Row centers from the content row top: title 15, album 28, artist 40.5, controls 57 (row heights 14/12/12/18, spacings 0/0.5/1.5). Fonts: system default design (NOT rounded). Title 11 semibold white; album 10 semibold white 62%; artist 9 regular white 45%; controls backward.fill / pause.fill|play.fill / forward.fill at 9pt, white 85%, centers 20 apart, first center at text origin + 10. No progress bar at rest: on hover a 3pt scrubber (hit height 12) fades in at y 69..72 of the text column without moving anything. Marquee with edge fade for long text.
- CALENDAR WIDGET (width 224): left padding 8. Month label (MMM, locale) 20 bold white, center y = 13.5. Week strip: 7 columns of 24, starting at x = 44, today is the 4th column (3 days before, 3 after); a partial next column fades out at the right edge and a partial previous column fades under the month label. Column header center y = 5: 8 semibold caps, white 40%; weekend letters red-tinted (r 0.84, g 0.38, b 0.38 at 75%). Number center y = 16: 11 medium white 55%; weekend red-tinted. The selected day (default today) shows the 3-letter header (MON) white 90% and its number at 17 semibold, center y = 18.5, systemBlue if it is today, white otherwise. Events area y = 36..72, left/right 8: up to 2 rows (3pt calendar-color bar, title 10 medium white 90%, time "9:00–10:00" or "All day" 9 regular white 50%), then "+N more" 9 white 45%. Empty state centered in the widget: calendar.badge.checkmark 10pt (white 45%) at y = 47.5 and "Nothing for today" 9.5 regular white 55% at y = 60.
